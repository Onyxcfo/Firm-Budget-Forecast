# Onyx Accounting Group — Budget 2027

`Firm_Budget_Forecast_2026.xlsx` — a chain. Each sheet feeds the next.

```
Billing Rates Updated  →  Bill Rates  →  Staff P&L  →  Monthly Capacity  →  Budget 2027
```

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Billing Rates Updated** | Your Sep–Oct 2026 export: teammate, client, hours, rate. The source of every bill rate. |
| **Bill Rates** | The 2026 client card, plus the block that prices **each person on their own billing** — hours × rate across every client they touched. |
| **Staff P&L** | Pay, employer tax, benefits (medical tier, PEO, 401k), loaded cost, COGS %, revenue, and gross profit on billable work — annual and monthly — by person. Tax rates and the PEO quote line by line on the same sheet. |
| **Monthly Capacity** | 2027 calendar, PTO, and an editable utilization per person on a **40-hour basis** (goal 35 of 40 = 87.5%) with Apr–Aug and Sep–Oct history beside it. Billable hours, revenue and pay by month. |
| **Budget 2027** | Monthly P&L, QuickBooks layout, twelve months plus total. |
| Owner Earnings | K-1 and tax off the 2027 budget, with the household income you entered. |
| Assumptions | Still pending your OK to delete — a one-screen index of where each item went. |
