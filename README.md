# Onyx Accounting Group — Budget 2027

`Firm_Budget_Forecast_2026.xlsx` — a chain. Each sheet feeds the next.

```
Bill Rates  →  Staff P&L  →  Monthly Capacity  →  Budget 2027
```

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Bill Rates** | The Sep–Oct 2026 billing export, with each person's weighted rate on top: $ billed ÷ hours billed. |
| **Staff P&L** | Pay, employer tax, benefits, loaded cost, COGS %, then each person's weighted rate with a **2027 increase by % or $**, hours, revenue and gross profit — annual and monthly. Payroll-tax rates and the Questco quote on the same sheet. |
| **Monthly Capacity** | Three blocks: the 2027 calendar; billable hours by month with delivery allocation, utilization and PTO as inputs per person; revenue by month. |
| **Budget 2027** | Monthly P&L, QuickBooks layout. Revenue and labour flow in; **overhead lines are blank for you to type**. |
| Owner Earnings | K-1 and tax off the 2027 budget. |
| Assumptions | Pending your OK to delete. |
