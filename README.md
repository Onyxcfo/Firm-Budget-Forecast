```
Billing Rates Updated  →  Bill Rates  →  Staff P&L  →  Monthly Capacity  →  Budget 2027
```

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Billing Rates Updated** | Your Sep–Oct 2026 export: teammate, client, hours, rate. The source of every bill rate. |
| **Bill Rates** | The 2026 client card, plus the block that prices **each person on their own billing**. |
| **Staff P&L** | Pay, employer tax, benefits, loaded cost, COGS %, revenue and gross profit — annual and monthly — by person. Payroll-tax rates and the Questco quote line by line on the same sheet. |

| **Budget 2027** | Monthly P&L, QuickBooks layout. Revenue and labour flow in; **overhead lines are blank for you to type**. |
| Owner Earnings | K-1 and tax off the 2027 budget, with the household income you entered. |
| Assumptions | Pending your OK to delete — an index of where each item went. |
