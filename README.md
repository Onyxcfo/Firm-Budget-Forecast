# Onyx Accounting Group — Budget 2027

`Firm_Budget_Forecast_2026.xlsx` — a chain. Each sheet feeds the next.

```
Bill Rates  →  Staff P&L  →  Monthly Capacity  →  Budget 2027
```

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Bill Rates** | The Sep–Oct 2026 billing export, with each person's weighted rate on top: $ billed ÷ hours billed. |
| **Staff P&L** | Pay, employer tax, benefits, loaded cost, COGS %, each person's weighted rate with a 2027 increase by % or $, hours, revenue and gross profit — annual and monthly. |
| **Monthly Capacity** | The 2027 calendar; billable hours by month with delivery allocation, utilization and PTO per person; revenue by month. |
| **Budget 2027** | Monthly P&L **on the QuickBooks chart of accounts**. Driven lines in black; every other account a blank yellow line to type. |
| Owner Earnings | K-1 and tax off the 2027 budget. |
| Assumptions | Pending your OK to delete. |

**Benefits are overhead, because that is where your chart puts them.** There is no benefits account
under Cost of Goods Sold; health insurance is `Insurance:Health Insurance`, the PEO fee is `Payroll
Processing Fees`. Staff P&L's COGS cost now excludes benefits to match, so gross profit ties.
`Admin Benefits` and `Officer Benefits` are left blank — use them if you book benefits there instead.

Holiday Pay and PTO are blank: holiday and PTO pay are already inside Wages and salaries.

Not budgeted — accounts with no place in a budget: the duplicate Billable Expense Income accounts,
Sales of Product Income, Services, Unapplied Cash, Uncategorized, Ask My Accountant, Reconciliation
Discrepancies, Bereavement Pay.
