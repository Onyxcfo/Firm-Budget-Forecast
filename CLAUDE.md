# Working rules for this repo — from Steven, keep across every session

- **Do only what is asked. Nothing extra.** No added tabs, blocks, rows, scenarios, toggles,
  reconciliations, notes or "improvements" that were not requested. If a request has an
  unavoidable consequence elsewhere, make the minimum change and say so in one line.
- **The latest copy Steven sends is the base.** His edits stand. Build on his file, not on
  the previous commit.
- **Keep it simple.** Four-sheet chain: Bill Rates → Staff P&L → Monthly Capacity → Budget 2027,
  plus Owner Earnings. Budget lines are the QuickBooks chart of accounts.
- **Overhead is his.** Never fill, spread or estimate overhead lines on the Budget.
- **Show the arithmetic, not a narrative.** When reporting numbers, give the before/after and
  what moved.
